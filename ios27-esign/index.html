Let's get you set up

A few steps. You're in.

Open this page on the iPhone or iPad you want to use. You'll need Wi-Fi and Developer Mode.

1

Get the two free apps

Download both apps from the App Store, then open LocalDevVPN and connect it.

MOpen MinisRuns the installerGET VLocalDevVPNConnect before setupGET
2

Open the ESign installer

Open the command in Open Minis, then press Enter in Shell Terminal. You can also copy and paste it yourself.

wget -qO- https://applejr.net/sideload/install.sh | sh

Open in Open MinisThe command comes from AppleJr's ESign installer.
3

Pair, sign in and install

Follow the setup on your device. Keep Open Minis and LocalDevVPN running until ESign is installed.

  1. In the installer, tap Start setup, then Start pairing.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode and follow the pairing prompt. Enable Developer Mode and restart if needed.
  3. Enter the pairing code shown in Open Minis. Keep its audio player open while pairing.
  4. Sign in with your Apple ID in the installer's local setup and complete any verification prompts. ESign will be signed and installed.

Good to know

Do I need a certificate?

You don't need to import an enterprise certificate. The installer uses your Apple ID to create personal signing credentials.

Will I need to refresh?

Apps signed with a free Apple ID generally need refreshing every seven days, and Apple's free-account limits still apply.

Which iOS versions work?

This installer is intended for iOS and iPadOS 27 or later. A Wi-Fi connection is required for setup.
